# Accessibility criteria and scoring rules

Three levels: **accessible**, **partially accessible**, **inaccessible**. The rules produce a
**suggestion** for reviewers; a reviewer always decides. Status: draft, to confirm with MOTIVAȚIE.

## Criteria (MOTIVAȚIE's form + 3 new options in bold)

| # | Criterion | Group | Main answer | Details | Required |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Access until entering the building | entry | Accessible / Inaccessible | Barriers: curb, stairs, other | mandatory |
| 2 | Ramp | entry | Yes / No / **Not needed (step-free entrance)** | Surface: pavement, concrete, wood, metal, handrail, metal wheel tracks, other | mandatory |
| 3 | Entrance doors | entry | Wide / Narrow | — | mandatory |
| 4 | Interior space | inside | Accessible / Inaccessible | — | mandatory |
| 5 | Adapted toilet | inside | Yes / No / **No public toilet** | — | mandatory |
| 6 | Elevator | inside | Yes / No / **Not needed** | — | optional |
| 7 | Interior doors | inside | Wide / Narrow | — | optional |
| 8 | Accessible checkout counter | inside | Yes / No / N/A | — | optional |
| 9 | Designated parking | parking | Yes (marked) / No (unmarked) | — | mandatory |

Every criterion also has a free-text notes question.

- **Not needed (ramp):** no steps or curb between street and door.
- **Not needed (elevator):** one floor, or everything the public uses is on the ground floor.
- **No public toilet:** the place has no toilet for visitors (pharmacy, kiosk).
- **Wide vs narrow:** threshold to confirm with MOTIVAȚIE; shown as a hint in the form.

## Scoring rules (suggestion)

**Step 1: Can you get in?** You can get in if you can reach the door **and** the entrance doors are wide.
Otherwise → **inaccessible**.

| Access until entering | Ramp | Can you reach the door? |
|---|---|---|
| Accessible | Not needed | Yes |
| Inaccessible (curb or stairs only) | Yes | Yes, the ramp bypasses them |
| Inaccessible (curb or stairs only) | No | No |
| Inaccessible ("other" barrier) | Any | No; reviewer reads the notes |
| Accessible | No or Yes | Contradictory → reviewer note |

**Step 2: Can you use it inside?** (interior space, toilet, elevator, interior doors, checkout)
- All answered inside criteria positive or not applicable → **accessible**
- Any negative (No / Narrow / Inaccessible) → **partially accessible**
- Optional criteria lower the level when negative; a missing optional answer does not.

**Parking never changes the level.** Missing parking is a separate marker and a reviewer note.

Every suggestion includes its reasons, e.g. "Partially accessible: no adapted toilet, narrow interior doors."

## Reviewer notes

| Note | When |
|---|---|
| No designated parking | Parking = No |
| Ramp makes the entrance passable — check photo | Stairs/curb + ramp Yes |
| Answers contradict each other | e.g. access Accessible but ramp No |
| "Other" barrier — read notes | Barrier = Other |
| Ramp with metal wheel tracks | Surface includes metal wheel tracks |
| Not enough to publish | Publishing rule not met |

## Publishing rule

| Level | Shown in the app when |
|---|---|
| Inaccessible | The entry path is known (criteria 1–3) |
| Partially accessible / Accessible | All 6 mandatory criteria are known |

Approved answers are always saved; until the rule is met the place stays "Not yet rated".
Information can be combined across several approved submissions. The reviewer has the final say.

## Photos

1–3 per assessed criterion (required when the feature exists). None for Not needed / No public
toilet / N/A. Public only after approval. Rejected submissions' photos deleted after 6 months.
